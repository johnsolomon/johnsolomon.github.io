I am a nuclear medicine resident physician at KPC Hemet Global Medical Center, where I completed my transitional year training (2024–2025) under the supervision of Dr. Sumanta Chaudhuri and program director Dr. Mindy Cheng. I earned my **MD from the American University of Antigua College of Medicine** (2024) and my **BA in Religious Studies from the University of California, Irvine** (2018), where I also conducted basic science research in neural circuit imaging at the UCI School of Medicine.

My clinical and research interests lie at the intersection of **diagnostic imaging, nuclear medicine, and interventional radiology**. I have published peer-reviewed work in *Cureus Journal of Medical Science* on metastatic melanoma recurrence and kyphoplasty cement embolism, as well as online case reports through the **American Osteopathic College of Radiology (AOCR)**. I also presented a poster at the **ACP Southern California Chapters Poster Competition** (2024) on uremic platelet dysfunction in an ESRD patient.

My background in **digital image processing** — honed through nearly a decade of work with SPSP Publications — has given me a precision-oriented eye for imaging that I bring into every scan interpretation. I believe the ability to see clearly, both literally and diagnostically, is a craft worth cultivating.

Prior to medical school, I volunteered in the emergency department at San Dimas Community Hospital, where a chance encounter with chest X-rays and a generous attending sparked my passion for radiology. I later served as a **Lead Teaching Assistant** at AUA, mentoring students from underrepresented backgrounds through their foundational sciences.

I am committed to practicing in the Pacific region, where I have deep **personal and cultural roots as a Coptic Egyptian and Assyrian American**. I am an active member of the **American College of Radiology**, **Radiological Society of North America (RSNA)**, **American Roentgen Ray Society**, and the **American Osteopathic College of Radiology**.

Outside medicine, I find balance in baking traditional **German sourdough rye bread** (Sauerteig-Roggenbrot) and unwinding over table tennis.
